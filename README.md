Workflow sandbox
===

A sandbox repo for trying out the auto-deletion workflows generated by gh-profiler.

The action in this repo will write gh-profiler output as a comment on any new issue or PR. When the issue or PR is closed, the profile output will be deleted (because it's no longer needed, and should be ephemeral), and the corresponding information in the Actions log will be deleted as well.

Try it out!
---

Feel free to [open an issue](https://github.com/ehmatthes/workflow_sandbox/issues/new), look at the profiler output, and then close the issue. The profile output should disappear when you close the issue.

Generate your own workflow
---

To generate a gh-profiler workflow for your project, see the [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/ehmatthes/gh-profiler#github-actions) section of the gh-profiler documentation.

You can also just copy any one of the workflows in the gh-profiler [workflow templates](https://github.com/ehmatthes/gh-profiler/tree/main/src/gh_profiler/templates) directory.
